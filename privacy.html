ClipFactory Privacy Policy

ClipFactory uses TikTok authentication and the TikTok Content Posting API to allow authorized users to publish video content.

ClipFactory only accesses information required to provide these features.

We do not sell personal information.

Users may revoke TikTok access at any time through their TikTok account settings.

For privacy or data deletion requests, contact the ClipFactory operator.
